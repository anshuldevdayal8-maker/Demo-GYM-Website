Built by lifters, for everyone

Forge opened in 2016 in a converted warehouse with twelve racks and one rule: no one trains alone. Ten years on, we are still an independent, member-first gym where beginners and competitive athletes share the same floor.

Our mission is simple: help you get stronger than you were last month, with coaching that fits your goals and a community that shows up.

Questions, answered

How does the free trial work?

Book any day and get seven days of full access. Bring ID and workout clothes. A trainer will walk you through the floor on day one.

Can I cancel my membership?

Yes. Monthly members cancel with 30 days' notice at the front desk or by email. Annual plans can be paused for up to 60 days.

What are your opening hours?

Monday to Saturday, 5:00 am to 11:00 pm. Sunday, 7:00 am to 8:00 pm. Holiday hours are posted a week ahead.

Do I need experience to join a class?

No. Every class has beginner options, and coaches adjust movements for injuries and different fitness levels.
